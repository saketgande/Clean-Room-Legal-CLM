How your document content is used

When you link a document, run Review, ask a question, or select "Explain selection," the relevant text is sent to Aegis's backend, which:

  • Stores it as a version/snapshot of a contract record belonging to your organization's Aegis account (contract records are scoped to your organization; other organizations on Aegis cannot see them).
  • Sends the relevant text to Anthropic's Claude API (a third-party AI service) to generate risk scores, identify clauses that deviate from your organization's playbooks, and answer your questions. Anthropic processes this text to generate a response; see Anthropic's privacy policy for how they handle it.
  • Does not use your document content to train any AI model.

Where information is stored

Contract content, versions, conversation history, and account information are stored in your organization's Aegis database, hosted at [hosting provider / region, e.g. "AWS us-east-1"]. Data is retained per your organization's Aegis configuration and is not shared with other Aegis customers. The access token and small local preferences described above are stored only in the browser storage of the device running Word, and are cleared when you sign out.

Third-party services

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — processes document/question text to generate AI review, risk, and chat responses, as described above.
  • Google Fonts — the panel loads the "Inter" typeface from Google's font CDN, which (like any web font request) receives the requesting device's IP address as part of that request.

We do not sell your information or your organization's contract data to third parties.

Your rights and choices

Access, correction, or deletion requests for data processed through the Add-in should go through your organization's Aegis administrator, since contract and account data belongs to your organization's Aegis tenant. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional rights (e.g., under GDPR or CCPA) — contact us at the address below with questions.

Data security

The access token used to authenticate API calls is short-lived; session renewal uses an HttpOnly cookie that isn't readable by any script running in the page. All communication between the Add-in and the backend is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Children's privacy

The Add-in is a business tool intended for use by professional employees of licensed organizations and is not directed at children.
