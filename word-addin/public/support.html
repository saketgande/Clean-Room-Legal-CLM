Common issues

  • "Add-in could not be loaded" or a certificate warning — fully quit and reopen Word, then try again.
  • Sign-in fails — confirm your Aegis account email and password are correct and that your account has been approved by your organization's Aegis administrator.
  • "Track changes" is greyed out — your version of Word doesn't support the feature set this requires; edits still apply, just without tracked-change markup.
  • "Insert redline" does nothing — the exact clause text couldn't be found in the document (it may have changed). Select the clause yourself, then try again.
  • A review or answer never finishes — check your network connection and try again; if it persists, contact us with the approximate time it happened.
