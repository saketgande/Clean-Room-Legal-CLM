Proposed order

How we'd do it

1
Counsel signs off the positions.

The items marked Lawyer: breach window, insurance amounts, SLA cap, arbitration seat and institution, data-breach super-cap, stamp duty, DPA transfers.

2
Fix templates and playbooks together.

One change per template–rule pair, then re-run the template check with every form option, not just the defaults. Done when every filled template passes its playbook with zero findings.

3
Build the per-type forms from what each template needs.

Every blank in a template has a question, and every question either fills the draft or says what it's for. A test keeps them in step.
