Where Ask Aegis falls short as an in-app agent

It answers general questions well. The gaps are in the finer details of acting inside the application: checking what already exists, filling every field, applying the same controls as the screens, and telling you what really happened. Every finding below has an example of what you type, what happens today, and what it should do.

Where the problems are

Open findings per area, split by how much damage they can do. High means a wrong action, a skipped control, or a false "done".

Example: one request, two ways in

The request form asks for everything Legal needs. The chat asked for four things and guessed the rest. That is the pattern behind many findings below.

Filed from the form

15 of 15 answers: entity, counterparty from the register, their signer, term, governing law, payment terms, start date, value…

Filed from the chat

4 of 15 answers: department, vendor name (typed), value, needed-by date. Everything else is missing, so Legal asks again.

Example: what happens when you say "approve it"

The screen runs three checks before an approval. The chat runs one.

Example: a contract made in the chat vs one uploaded

The upload route files a request and starts a workflow. A contract drafted in the chat stops after the file is saved.

Suggested order of work

Grouped so each wave can be built and checked on its own.

WAVE 1 · STOP WRONG ACTIONS

Controls the screens already have

  • Open-risk check and authority on approve and sign
  • Stage guard before edit or redraft
  • Honest "done" for advance and start workflow
  • Handle vs id on comment, signature, negotiation
WAVE 2 · COMPLETE RECORDS

Everything the chat creates

  • Workflow for chat drafts
  • Counterparty and entity on drafts
  • Full form fields on requests
  • Remove unsafe request labels
WAVE 3 · TRUTHFUL LOOKUPS

What the model sees

  • Today's date in the prompt
  • find_contracts, approvals, requests, renewals
  • Counts that say when a list was cut
WAVE 4 · ENGINE

Confirmations and long chats

  • Parallel tool calls, two-step approve
  • Readable cards, reject with a reason
  • Newest messages, step limit message
WAVE 5 · NEW ABILITIES

What users will ask next

  • Request actions, notices, notifications
  • Approver and authority lookups
  • Teams, users, counterparty register
