7.1 No change to the scope, Deliverables, due dates, Key Personnel or Fees of this SOW is effective unless it is set out in a written change order signed by authorised representatives of both Parties (a "Change Order").

7.2 Either Party may request a change in writing. Within five (5) working days of a request, the Provider shall provide a written impact assessment describing the effect of the change on the scope, Deliverables, due dates and Fees.

7.3 Until a Change Order is signed by both Parties, the Provider shall continue to perform this SOW unchanged. The Provider is not entitled to any additional payment or extension of time for work performed, or delay incurred, without a signed Change Order.

7.4 Instructions given orally, by email or in meetings do not change this SOW unless and until they are recorded in a signed Change Order.
