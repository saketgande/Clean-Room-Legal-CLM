NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and the disclosure of Confidential Information, the Parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Disclosing Party" means the Party that discloses Confidential Information, directly or through its Representatives, and "Receiving Party" means the Party that receives it, in each case as identified in the Recitals.

1.2 "Representatives" means a Party's directors, officers and employees, its Affiliates and their directors, officers and employees, its professional advisers, and its contractors and service providers, in each case who need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose and are bound by written (or professional) obligations of confidentiality no less protective than this Agreement.

1.3 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

1.4 "Purpose" has the meaning given in Recital A.

2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

2.1 "Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, before or after the Effective Date, in any form or medium (whether written, oral, electronic, visual or by demonstration or inspection), including without limitation technical, financial, business, operational, commercial and personnel information, trade secrets, know-how, inventions, source code, product plans, business plans, pricing, financial data, customer and supplier lists, AI/ML training data, model weights, datasets, and any outputs or derivatives thereof, regardless of whether such information is marked or identified as "confidential."

2.2 Confidential Information expressly includes: (a) all data, datasets, and information used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any artificial intelligence or machine learning model; (b) outputs, embeddings, or derivative works generated using Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; and (c) the fact that discussions between the parties are occurring.

2.3 Confidential Information also includes all notes, analyses, compilations, summaries, forecasts and other materials prepared by or for the Receiving Party or its Representatives that contain, reflect or are derived from any Confidential Information, and the existence and terms of this Agreement.

3. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

3.1 The obligations of confidentiality do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show, by its written records: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives; (b) was rightfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure, free of any obligation of confidence; (c) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction and without breach of any obligation of confidentiality owed to the Disclosing Party; or (d) is independently developed by or for the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information.

3.2 The Receiving Party bears the burden of showing, by its written records, that an exclusion applies. A combination of elements is not excluded merely because individual elements of it are publicly available or in the Receiving Party's possession.

3.3 No right to use information retained in the unaided memory of any person is granted under this Agreement.

4. PERMITTED USE AND STANDARD OF CARE

4.1 The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

4.2 The Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of similar sensitivity, and no less than reasonable care.

4.3 The Receiving Party shall not use Confidential Information to train, fine-tune, test, or otherwise develop any artificial intelligence or machine learning model, algorithm, or system. This does not prevent the Receiving Party from handling Confidential Information for the Purpose with business software and services (including AI-enabled tools) whose provider is bound by confidentiality and does not use the Confidential Information to train or improve any model.

4.4 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to its Representatives, and is responsible for any act or omission of a Representative that would be a breach of this Agreement if done by the Receiving Party.

4.5 The Receiving Party shall not copy or reproduce Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for the Purpose, and shall not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any prototype, software, sample or other tangible object embodying Confidential Information.

4.6 The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party in writing promptly and without undue delay after becoming aware of any actual or suspected unauthorised access to, or use or disclosure of, Confidential Information, and shall cooperate with the Disclosing Party to regain possession of it and prevent any further unauthorised use or disclosure.

5. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE AND LEGAL PROCESS

5.1 If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives receives any subpoena, court order, regulatory demand or other legal process requiring disclosure of any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall: (a) provide prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party prior to any such disclosure to the extent permitted by law; (b) cooperate fully with the Disclosing Party in seeking a protective order, confidential treatment, or other appropriate relief; and (c) disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that is legally required to be disclosed.

5.2 Where applicable law prohibits notice before disclosure, the Receiving Party shall give notice as soon as legally permissible. No notice is needed for a routine examination by a regulator or a requirement of a stock exchange that is not targeted at the Disclosing Party or its information, provided the Receiving Party asks for confidential treatment of any Confidential Information disclosed.

5.3 Any disclosure made in compliance with this Section 5 shall not cause the information disclosed to cease to be Confidential Information for any other purpose, and all other obligations under this Agreement shall continue to apply to it.

6. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF INFORMATION

6.1 Within ten (10) business days of expiration or termination of this Agreement, or of the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information (including all copies, extracts and derivatives) held by it or its Representatives, and certify in writing, signed by an authorised representative, that it has done so.

6.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies (a) that it is required to keep by applicable law or regulation, or (b) held in automated backup systems that are not accessible in the ordinary course of business and are overwritten in the normal backup cycle. Copies kept under this Section 6.2 remain subject to this Agreement for as long as they are kept, notwithstanding any expiry of it.

7.2 The Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality shall survive expiration or termination of this Agreement and shall {survival_clause} after such expiration or termination with respect to general Confidential Information.

7.3 Information that is a trade secret remains protected for so long as it remains a trade secret, or otherwise remains protectable as confidential information, under applicable law.

7.4 Subject to Sections 7.2 and 7.3, Sections 2 to 6 and 8 to 11, and any other provision which by its nature is intended to survive, survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

8. REMEDIES AND INJUNCTIVE RELIEF

8.1 Each Party acknowledges that a breach or threatened breach of this Agreement may cause the other Party irreparable harm for which damages would not be an adequate remedy, and that either Party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief, to the extent permitted by applicable law without the requirement to post a bond or other security, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity. The rights and remedies under this Agreement are cumulative.

8.2 No limitation or exclusion of liability in this Agreement or in any other agreement between the Parties shall apply to, or cap, a Party's liability for breach of its confidentiality obligations under this Agreement.

10.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any right, title, interest or licence, by implication, estoppel or otherwise, in or to any Confidential Information or any patent, copyright, trade secret, trademark or other intellectual property right of the Disclosing Party, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose in accordance with this Agreement.

10.2 All Confidential Information is provided "as is." The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of any Confidential Information, and shall have no liability arising from its use, except as may be set out in a definitive written agreement between the Parties.

10.3 Neither Party is obliged by this Agreement to disclose any particular information, to continue discussions, or to enter into any further agreement or transaction. Any such obligation will arise only under a definitive written agreement signed by both Parties.

10.4 Nothing in this Agreement restricts either Party from independently developing or acquiring products or services similar to those discussed, provided it does so without use of the other Party's Confidential Information.

11. GENERAL

11.1 Personal Data. {nda_personal_data} Each Party shall notify the other without undue delay of any personal data breach affecting the other Party's Confidential Information.

11.2 Export Control. Each Party shall comply with all applicable export control and sanctions laws in relation to any Confidential Information it receives.

11.3 No Publicity. Neither Party shall make any public announcement concerning the Purpose or this Agreement, or use the other Party's name or marks, without the other Party's prior written consent.

11.4 Notices. All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognized courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the address for notices of the receiving Party set out in the signature blocks below or such other address as a Party notifies in writing. A notice is effective on receipt.

11.5 Assignment. Neither Party may assign, transfer or delegate this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business, on written notice to the other Party. Any purported assignment in breach of this Section is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

11.6 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings, agreements and communications, whether written or oral, regarding that subject matter.

11.7 Amendment and Waiver. This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it, and no waiver is effective unless in writing.

11.8 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

11.9 Relationship. Nothing in this Agreement creates any partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the Parties.

11.10 Counterparts and Electronic Signature. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered electronically or by an electronic signature service, including electronic signatures under the Information Technology Act, 2000, are binding as originals.
