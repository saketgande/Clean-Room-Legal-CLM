8.1 The Provider shall hold and maintain throughout the Subscription Term a current ISO/IEC 27001 certification or a SOC 2 Type II report covering the Service, issued by an independent auditor, and shall provide the Customer with a copy of the current certificate or report annually and on request.

8.3 The Provider shall notify the Customer of any Security Incident without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours after becoming aware of it. 8.4 Each Party shall comply with Data Protection Laws. 8.5 The Provider shall ensure that all personnel with access to Customer Data are bound by confidentiality obligations and have received appropriate data protection and security training.
