What Aegis is

Contract lifecycle management, with AI built into every step.

Aegis is a contract‑first legal platform. You bring contracts in, and Aegis reviews them for risk, checks them against your negotiation playbooks, routes them for approval and signature, then tracks the obligations and renewals that follow. The same intelligence is available right inside Microsoft Word through the Aegis add‑in — so reviewing and redlining never means leaving your document.

Intelligence

06 · Playbooks

Turn your negotiation standards into rules Aegis can check automatically.
6

Playbooks & the AI builder AI

House positions, applied to any contract
/playbooks · /playbooks/build

A playbook is a set of clause‑level rules — your preferred position, prohibited language, required terms and negotiation guidance. Create one by hand, or use the interactive AI builder: attach an exemplar contract or template, then chat to refine (“be strict on liability,” “we're the vendor”). When you run a playbook against a contract, Aegis surfaces deviations and proposes redlines to bring it back in line.

Generate a playbook from an exemplar with AI
Publish versions; keep a clean history
Run against a contract → deviations + fixes
Insights — which clauses deviate most often

07 · Approvals

Route a contract to the right people, automatically, before it can be signed.
7

Approvals

Requests · Routing rules · Approver groups
/approvals · /approve/{token}

Define routing rules that match contracts by type, value and risk, then send them to the right approver group in sequence — any‑one or all‑must‑approve. Approvers decide in‑app, or via a one‑time email link with no login required. On final approval, the contract advances toward signature.

Requests — approve / reject with a comment
Routing rules — criteria → approver chain
Approver groups — e.g. Senior Counsel, Finance
Email decisions via secure token links
8

Signatures

Send for e‑signature & track to completion
/signatures

Send the authoritative version for e‑signature (DocuSign), assign signers and fields, and set a deadline. Track each envelope as it moves Sent → Viewed → Signed → Completed, with status synced automatically. When everyone has signed, the contract advances to Active.

Send to recipients with fields & deadline
Track envelope & per‑signer progress
Resend, void or cancel envelopes
Download signed PDF & audit trail

Contract Brain AI

Portfolio‑wide Q&A with citations
/brain

Ask questions across your whole portfolio — or scope to a project or contract — and get an answer grounded in specific documents. Counting questions are answered straight from the database (no guesswork); text questions use retrieval over your clause library, every answer backed by citations.

Ask: “How many vendor contracts have a 2‑year initial term?” · “List every limitation‑of‑liability clause we've agreed above our cap.”

Tabular Review AI

A spreadsheet, filled in by AI
/tabular-reviews

Pick a set of contracts and define the columns you care about (Liability cap, Term, Renewal clause…). Aegis extracts each value for every contract — rows are contracts, columns are your questions — and flags cells that need a human look. Export the finished grid to spreadsheet.

Select contracts & define columns
